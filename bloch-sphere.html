Legal ranges are enforced: the state angles live on θ ∈ [0°, 180°] and φ ∈ [0°, 360°), which covers the sphere exactly once. The three rotation angles wrap freely on [−180°, 180°]. Once any of them is non-zero the unrotated |ψ⟩ stays on the sphere as a dashed marker, so the arrow the equation describes is always still visible.
